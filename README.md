# 📊 Z-Lite Electronics Sales Analysis

## 🧩 Problem Statement

The Z-Lite electronics dataset contains sales and pricing information with structural inconsistencies. 
This project focuses on cleaning the dataset and uncovering insights into product performance, pricing patterns, and revenue distribution across regions.



## 🎯 Objectives

* Clean and standardize the dataset
* Analyze pricing and product trends
* Examine revenue trends across regions
* Generate actionable business insights



## 🧹 Data Cleaning

* Standardized the date column to ensure consistency
* Corrected spelling inconsistencies across the dataset
* Identified and resolved pricing and categorization issues for products such as Laptop A15 and Monitor M24 to ensure accurate analysis



## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Key questions explored:

* What is the average product price?
* What is the total number of units sold per product?
* How is revenue distributed across products?



## 📊 Analysis

Using Excel pivot tables, the dataset was analyzed to identify:

* Revenue contribution by product
* Units sold across different products
* Regional sales performance trends



## 📈 Key Insights

* Revenue is concentrated among a few products, while others contribute minimally
* A noticeable decline in revenue occurred over time, reaching its lowest point in March across all regions
*  TV 43Q is the most demanded product in most regions (North, West, and South), while  Camera C100 leads in the East region



## 💡 Recommendations

* Conduct customer surveys to better understand preferences and improve customer experience
* Increase advertising efforts to boost awareness across underperforming regions
* Focus investment on high-demand products within each region
